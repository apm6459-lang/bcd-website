Chapter 7 Bankruptcy

Understanding the Chapter 7 Process

Chapter 7 is a type of bankruptcy commonly referred to as liquidation. Unlike Chapter 13, it does not involve creating a repayment plan.

A bankruptcy trustee may gather and sell property that is not protected by applicable exemptions and use the proceeds to pay creditors. Many individual Chapter 7 cases, however, are no-asset cases in which there is no distribution to unsecured creditors.

For individuals who qualify, Chapter 7 may result in the discharge of certain debts and provide an opportunity for a financial fresh start.

Source: U.S. Courts — Chapter 7 Bankruptcy Basics
