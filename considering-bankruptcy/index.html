Considering Bankruptcy

Clear information to help you understand your options

Financial problems can feel overwhelming, and bankruptcy is only one possible option. Understanding how bankruptcy works, what it may accomplish, and what alternatives may be available can help you make a more informed decision.

This section explains the basics of bankruptcy, Chapter 7 and Chapter 13, potential advantages and disadvantages, alternatives to filing, and what generally happens if a bankruptcy case is filed.
