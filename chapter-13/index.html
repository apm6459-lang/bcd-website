Chapter 13 Bankruptcy

Repayment and reorganization over time.

Chapter 13 bankruptcy provides for the adjustment of debts of an individual with regular income. It allows a debtor to keep property while paying debts over time, usually over a period of three to five years.

Chapter 13 is sometimes called a "wage earner's plan." Individuals propose a repayment plan that provides for payments to creditors over time. A Chapter 13 trustee evaluates the case, collects plan payments, and makes distributions to creditors.

Chapter 13 may also provide individuals with opportunities to address certain secured debts and, in some circumstances, cure delinquent mortgage payments over time. The treatment of debts and property depends on the individual's case and the terms of the court-approved plan.

Source: U.S. Courts - Chapter 13 Bankruptcy Basics
