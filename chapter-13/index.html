Chapter 13 Bankruptcy

Understanding the Repayment Plan Process

Chapter 13 bankruptcy allows an individual with regular income to develop a plan to repay all or part of their debts over time. Repayment plans generally run for three to five years.

During this process, a Chapter 13 trustee receives plan payments and distributes them to creditors according to the confirmed plan.

Source: U.S. Courts — Chapter 13 Bankruptcy Basics
