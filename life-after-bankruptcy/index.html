FRESH START BASICS

Moving Forward After Bankruptcy

Review Your Credit Reports

Your credit reports contain information about your borrowing and payment history. Reviewing them can help you identify information that may be inaccurate or incomplete.

Learn about credit reports →

Use New Credit Carefully

If you begin using credit again, focus on manageable payments, on-time payment history, and avoiding unnecessary applications for multiple accounts at once.

Vehicle Financing

Financing may be available after bankruptcy, but terms can vary. Before agreeing to a loan, understand the payment, interest rate, loan term, total cost, and whether payment activity is reported to credit reporting companies.

Prepare for Homeownership

Bankruptcy does not necessarily mean homeownership is permanently out of reach. Eligibility and waiting periods can depend on the loan program, bankruptcy chapter, discharge or dismissal date, credit history, and other factors.

Build a Budget & Emergency Savings

A realistic budget can help you plan for regular bills, manage spending, and begin setting money aside for unexpected expenses and future goals.

Be Careful With Fresh-Start Offers

After bankruptcy, you may receive offers for credit and other financial products. Compare costs and terms carefully, and be cautious of anyone promising a quick or guaranteed credit fix.
